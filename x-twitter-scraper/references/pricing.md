# Billing And Usage

Pricing, included usage, and operation costs can change. Read the current
[billing guide](https://docs.xquik.com/guides/billing) before quoting any amount
or estimating a workflow.

## Rules

- Retrieve current prices and operation costs before presenting them.
- Show the exact amount, target, and operation before requesting approval.
- Require explicit approval before billing actions or metered bulk jobs.
- Never infer approval from prior purchases or unrelated instructions.
- Never retry a failed billing action without new approval.
- Keep plan changes and account funding in the Xquik dashboard.

## Estimates

Use supported estimate endpoints before extractions, draws, monitors, and other
large workflows. Present the returned estimate without applying cached prices.
